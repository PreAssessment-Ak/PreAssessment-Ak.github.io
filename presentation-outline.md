# Presentation Outline — ABC Tutoring Website

**Format:** 3–5 slides, PDF
**Audience:** Dana (non-technical business owner)
**Goal:** Show Dana what you built, why, and what she can learn from it

---

## Slide 1 — What We Heard From You
- You needed to move off Facebook and reduce phone bookings
- Parents should be able to browse tutors and book directly
- Clean, friendly design for a parent audience
- No payment needed — you invoice separately

## Slide 2 — What We Built
- Home page introducing ABC Tutoring
- Tutor listing with all 6 tutors (photo, subjects, rate, availability)
- Booking flow: pick a slot → fill in info → instant confirmation
- Booked slots automatically disappear so no double-bookings
- Email notification to Dana every time a booking is made

## Slide 3 — Understanding Your Visitors (PostHog)
- Explain in plain English what PostHog does
- Show the two metrics Dana asked for:
  - Which tutor profiles parents click most
  - Which subjects parents are most interested in
- Include a screenshot of the live PostHog dashboard

## Slide 4 — Live Dashboard
- Screenshot or link to the shared PostHog dashboard
- Point out specific insights from the simulated traffic
- Frame it as: "Here's what you'll see after real parents start visiting"

## Slide 5 — Next Steps
- Dana provides real tutor photos → swap placeholders
- Set up EmailJS with her real email address
- Share the site link with parents
- Optional future additions: testimonials page, contact form, Google search optimization

---

## Tone Notes
- No jargon — write as if Dana has never used analytics software
- Focus on business value, not technical implementation
- Use simple visuals and screenshots over text-heavy slides
